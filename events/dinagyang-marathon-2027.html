About the Event

The Iloilo Dinagyang Marathon Heritage Run – 4th Edition, organized by Velocity 5000, is Iloilo's flagship road running festival that celebrates fitness, culture, and heritage. Inspired by the vibrant spirit of the Dinagyang Festival, the marathon takes runners on a scenic journey through the city's historic landmarks and modern attractions while showcasing the warmth and hospitality of the Ilonggo community.

Featuring race categories of 5K, 10K, 21K (Half Marathon), and 42K (Full Marathon), the event welcomes beginners, recreational runners, and seasoned marathoners alike. More than just a race, it is a celebration of community, perseverance, and sports tourism, bringing together runners from across the Philippines and beyond.

Race Guide

Registration & Eligibility

  • All runners must complete the official registration form.
  • Participants below 18 years old must submit a signed waiver from a parent or legal guardian.
  • By registering, participants confirm that they are medically fit to participate and agree to the event waiver.

Race Bib & Attire

  • The official Iloilo Dinagyang Marathon singlet is encouraged.
  • Your race bib must be worn clearly on the front of your body at all times.
  • Participants without a visible bib may be removed from the course.

Race Start & Timing

  • Participants must start with their assigned wave.
  • Late starters may be disqualified.
  • Overall winners will be determined using Official Gun Time.

Safety & Medical

  • Always follow instructions from race officials, marshals, and medical personnel.
  • If advised by medical staff to stop, please prioritize your safety.
  • Race routes may be adjusted if necessary for safety reasons.

Awards & Recognition

  • Winners must be present during the awarding ceremony.
  • Unclaimed prizes may be forfeited.
  • Finisher shirts will be issued only in the size selected during registration. Size exchanges are not guaranteed.

Event Policies

  • Photos and videos taken during the event may be used by the organizers for promotional, marketing, and documentation purposes.
  • The event will proceed rain or shine unless safety conditions require postponement.
  • Registration fees are non-refundable.
